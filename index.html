FAQs

How long does shipping take?+
Orders are processed within 1–2 business days. US standard delivery is typically 3–7 business days after dispatch. Free on orders over $150. Full details on our Fulfillment Policy.
What's your return policy?+
We accept returns within 30 days of delivery on unworn, unwashed items with tags attached. See our Refund Policy for full details.
How do the sizes fit?+
Most pieces are cut with a modern boxy/oversized fit. Each product page lists exact sizing. When in doubt, size down for a fitted look or stay true-to-size for the intended drape.
Are your products authentic?+
Every item is an original Reserved LA design, produced in-house. We are not a reseller — everything ships directly from us to you.
