Our Story

Built in Los Angeles for people who want their staples to actually last.

Reserved LA started with a simple frustration: streetwear had gotten loud, thin, and overpriced. We wanted the opposite — heavyweight essentials with clean lines, honest fabrics, and details that hold up wash after wash.

Every piece in our collection is designed in-house in Los Angeles. We obsess over fabric weight, fit, and finish — from 420gsm brushed fleece on our hoodies to 14oz rigid denim cut for a true baggy fit. We're not a reseller and we don't drop filler. We make things we actually want to wear, then we make them properly.

What We Stand For

  • Quality first. Premium, heavyweight fabrics and construction you can feel the moment you put it on.
  • Considered fits. Modern boxy and oversized silhouettes that look intentional, not accidental.
  • Fair pricing. Direct-to-you means no middle-man markup on genuinely good pieces.
  • Real support. A responsive team that answers your emails — usually the same day.
Reserved LA is a brand operated by ZA Distribution LLC, a California limited liability company. Every order is fulfilled and supported directly by our team — you can reach us anytime at support@reservedla.com.
